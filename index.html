How to write sections and notes

A line starting with # starts a section. Add the minutes after a comma. Every line under it is an exercise.

The type of section sets the rules. It comes from the name (Stick: ladder, Sync: four-way, Carmine, groove or fill: groove), or write it after a comma: ladder, four-way, groove or routine.

A routine is a checklist (hand speed, warm-ups). Each line is Name 80 BPM 2 min: details. Tempo and time are optional. With a time the item moves on by itself. With a tempo the metronome plays it after a bar of count-in and adds 1 BPM after every 4 clean bars. Press Mistake and those 4 bars start again. Next session the item starts 1 BPM above where it started this time. If every item has a time, the section runs for their total.

Under an exercise, add one line per instrument to show its notes. One character per note position, - is a rest, | splits bars.

# Carmine, 15 min
Carmine groove p20 #1
HH x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-
SD ----o-------o---
BD o-------o-------

Instruments: HH hi-hat, CY ride, CR crash, SD, HT, MT, FT toms, BD kick, HF hi-hat foot. Use 4, 8 or 16 characters per bar (quarters, eighths, sixteenths). Instruments in the same bar can use different lengths. g is a ghost note, o on HH is open. 4/4 only.

Triplets: write 12 characters per bar (three per beat, eighth-note triplets) or 24 (six per beat). They get a bracket with a 3. A beat is a triplet beat as soon as one note falls off the straight grid, so keep each beat either straight or triplet.

HH x-xx-xx-xx-x
SD ---o-----o--
BD o-----o-----

Sticking: write r or l instead of o, and b for both hands. The letters appear under the notes (R in black, L in gold).

Stick Control p5 #1 singles
SD rlrlrlrl

One rep is one pass of the notes, one bar at 4/4 (two bars if you write two). The boxes fill by themselves as the click runs.
